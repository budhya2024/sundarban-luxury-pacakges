# Sundarban Luxury Package (sundarbanluxurypackage.com)

> Premier Luxury Travel & Tour Agency for Exclusive Sundarban National Park Safaris, Private Boat Cruises, and Heritage Resort Stays in West Bengal, India.

## About Sundarban Luxury Package
Sundarban Luxury Package is a specialized eco-tour operator offering premium, comfortable, and personalized travel experiences to the Sundarbans — the world's largest mangrove forest and home to the Royal Bengal Tiger. We cater to families, couples, corporate groups, wildlife photographers, and international tourists who seek natural adventure without compromising on comfort and luxury.

---

## Core Services & Offerings
- **Private & Deluxe Boat Safaris:** Exclusive air-conditioned and open-deck luxury vessels with safety equipment, dining area, and dedicated local crew.
- **Luxury Resort Accommodations:** Premium, air-conditioned river-facing suites and eco-resorts with modern amenities.
- **AC Transport Pick-up & Drop:** Private AC vehicle transfers from Kolkata (Airport, Howrah/Sealdah Railway Stations, or Hotels) to Godkhali Ferry Ghat.
- **Gourmet Bengali & Continental Dining:** Freshly prepared local cuisine, seafood delicacies, and custom meal plans served onboard and at resorts.
- **Guided Wildlife & Cultural Expeditions:** Expert government-certified wildlife naturalists, forest permit handling, and evening local folk cultural performances (Bonbibi Pala / Jhumur Dance).

## Major Attractions Covered
- **Watchtowers:** Sajnekhali, Sudhanyakhali, Dobanki (Canopy Walk), Burirdabri, Netidhopani.
- **Wildlife & Sanctuaries:** Sajnekhali Bird Sanctuary, Bhagabatpur Crocodile Project, Dobanki Deer Park.
- **Rivers & Estuaries:** Matla, Bidya, Gomar, Durgaduni, and 5-River Junction (Mouth of the Bay of Bengal).
- **Heritage & Culture:** Hamilton Bungalow, Rabindranath Tagore Heritage Site, Local Craft Villages.

## Key Booking & Practical Information

### Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** October to March (Pleasant weather, ideal for wildlife sightings and birdwatching).
- **Monsoon & Off-Peak:** July to September (Lush greenery, peaceful, subject to weather conditions).

### Typical Safety & Onboard Standards
- Lifejackets and lifebuoys for all passengers.
- Fire extinguishers and first-aid facilities onboard.
- Experienced local boat captains familiar with tidal channels.
- Experienced English, Hindi, and Bengali speaking wildlife guides.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: Where does the tour start from?**  
A: Tours typically start from Kolkata with pickup from the Airport, Railway Stations (Howrah/Sealdah), or hotels/residences via private AC cars to Godkhali Jetty.

**Q: Are meals included in the luxury package?**  
A: Yes, all packages include all major meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Afternoon Snacks, and Dinner). Both Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian options are provided with a focus on fresh Bengali cuisine.

**Q: Are Royal Bengal Tigers guaranteed to be seen?**  
A: While the Sundarbans is home to the Royal Bengal Tiger, wildlife sightings depend on nature and luck. Our experienced guides navigate the best spots like Sudhanyakhali and Dobanki to maximize sighting chances.
