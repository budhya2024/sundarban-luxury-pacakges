# Sundarban Luxury Package

> Premier luxury travel and tour agency offering exclusive Sundarban National Park safaris, private boat cruises, and heritage resort stays in West Bengal, India.

Sundarban Luxury Package is a specialized eco-tour operator offering premium, comfortable, and personalized travel experiences to the Sundarbans — the world's largest mangrove forest and a UNESCO World Heritage site, home to the Royal Bengal Tiger. The agency caters to families, couples, corporate retreats, wildlife photographers, and international travelers seeking natural adventure with high-end hospitality.

Key offerings include private air-conditioned and open-deck luxury safari vessels, 5-star river-facing resort accommodations (including Hotel Sonar Bangla), roundtrip AC vehicle transfers from Kolkata, gourmet Bengali and continental dining, government-certified wildlife naturalists, and complete forest permit handling.

Peak travel season runs from October to March with optimum weather and tiger/bird sightings; lush monsoon expeditions run from July to September. All tours depart from Kolkata (Airport, Howrah/Sealdah railway stations, or hotels) via dedicated private AC vehicle transfers directly to Godkhali Ferry Ghat.

## Major Attractions & Watchtowers
- [Sudhanyakhali Watchtower](https://sundarbanluxurypackage.com/tour-details): Most renowned watchtower for spotting Royal Bengal Tigers, spotted deer, monitor lizards, and wild boars around the freshwater pond.
- [Dobanki Watchtower & Canopy Walk](https://sundarbanluxurypackage.com/tour-details): Elevated 496-meter caged aerial canopy walkway over the mangrove forest canopy.
- [Sajnekhali Watchtower & Bird Sanctuary](https://sundarbanluxurypackage.com/tour-details): Forest Department head office, mangrove interpretation museum, and crocodile breeding pond.
- [Rivers, Estuaries & Creeks](https://sundarbanluxurypackage.com/gallery): Silent cruising through Matla, Bidya, Gomar, and the 5-River confluence facing the Bay of Bengal.
- [Heritage Sites & Local Culture](https://sundarbanluxurypackage.com/about): Sir Daniel Hamilton estate, Rabindranath Tagore heritage bungalow, and evening Bonbibi Pala folk performances.
